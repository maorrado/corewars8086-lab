# m050-XORB paired extension against five e1p3 copies

This is a post-hoc, paired extension of the frozen five-identical-e1p3 density
stress. It adds only the exact m050-XORB arm (m050 A plus the previously built
one-byte B-worker `XOR [BX],BP` substitution) on the same 40 K/cohort cells,
seeds, names, orientations, opponents, Zombies, and original engine. Existing
m050 results are the same-seed controls; they are not new samples. Total added
work is 800 wars. This is targeted mechanism evidence, not a fresh holdout or
general-performance promotion test.

Report paired deltas for each K and the exact hypergeometrically weighted
five-copy pool. The uncertainty unit is the ten cohort clusters per K, after
averaging the two name orientations. Use t(df=9)=2.262157 and label intervals
nominal/descriptive. Do not treat 80 configs as 80 independent cohorts or
reuse the result as evidence that XOR-B is broadly better. Preserve the
parent experiment and all partial output. Do not modify `final/`, branch, or
remote state.
