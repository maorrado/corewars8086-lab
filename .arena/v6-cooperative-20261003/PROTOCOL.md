# Cooperative V6 Arena, 2026-10-03

User override of Arena: three independent strategy cards, all agents stay active,
measured verified improvements are broadcast and become a shared baseline.
`bracket.py` is used to deal cards/identical task briefs; its generic elimination
workflow is not claimed to be completed. Exact simulation, not prose judging,
decides baseline changes. Main agent coordinates benchmarks and independent
cross-reviews.

Frozen original V6: A 221/B 202 bytes, original friend provenance retained.
All writes and runner outputs are session-local. 75 published 2025 online teams
and four 2025 live Zombies are copied with hashes. This pool is not called the
actual 2025 final or a guarantee for the unknown 2026 competition.

Stages: independent source variants; exact assembly, 256-byte limit and binary
deduplication; 200-battle exploration per arm; 1000-battle refined exploration
for top candidates; select ONE candidate before fresh 3000-battle holdout;
if positive, repeat with a separate 3000-battle holdout; primary difference
versus original V6 is paired per cohort/seed; inspect per-warrior scores and
modern-rival/no-Zombie stress. For multiple confirmation selections, use a
new untouched confirmation or acknowledge/correct multiplicity.

Broadcast only after both fresh holdouts point positive and pooled 95% paired
interval excludes zero. A candidate chosen after failed holdout uses new seeds
for its own confirmation; do not recycle failed holdout as fresh confirmation.
Do not require domination of every matchup: material targeted weaknesses are
reported and weighed alongside the general gain. Do not claim universal best.
Any new shared-best revision records exact binary hashes and evidence paths.

Persistent A2Batch uses unmodified deterministic v6 JAR, all arms are loaded
as CAND on identical explicit three-opponent cohorts/seeds. Frozen input guards
refuse mismatched cache reuse. Decisive runs are checked against cold original
CLI output. Existing unrelated agent2 jobs are never interrupted.

Future extensions are driven by measured results and reviewer suggestions;
close the session after meaningful follow-up batches and confirmation rather
than falsely claiming theoretical maximum or searching indefinitely.
